# My Favorite Show: Megamind
### *Genre: Superhero / Comedy / Sci-Fi*
---
## Key Characters
1. **Megamind:** main alien in the story finding meaning beyond superficial roles between good and evil
2. **Minion:** Megamind's loyal robo-fish cyborg assistant since birth
3. **Roxanne Ritchi:** a sassy journalist trying to uncover the truth and gets hassled in between
4. **Metro Man:** the original hero in the story who staged his death to escape societal pressure
5. **Hal Stewart/Tighten/Titan:** the actual villain who turns destructive out of jealousy and entitlement
