# _My Favorite Show: Megamind_
## *Genre: Superhero / Comedy / Sci-Fi*

## Key Characters
* **Megamind:** main alien in the story finding meaning beyond superficial roles between good and evil
* **Minion:** Megamind's loyal robo-fish cyborg assistant since birth
* **Roxanne Ritchi:** a sassy journalist trying to uncover the truth while getting hassled in between
* **Metro Man:** the original hero in the story who staged his death to escape societal pressure
* **Hal Stewart/Tighten/Titan:** the actual villain who turns destructive out of jealousy and entitlement
